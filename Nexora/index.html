STATUTORY REGULATORY FRAMEWORK

UGC Guidelines for Online Degrees

Official University Grants Commission (UGC-DEB) rules governing entitlement, legal degree parity, permitted courses, and fee protection in India.

📜

100% Legal & Academic Parity

Under UGC Gazette Notification (File No. 1-9/2018), degrees awarded through Online Mode by UGC-DEB entitled HEIs carry 100% legal parity with campus degrees for UPSC, Govt Job Exams, and Global Higher Education.

🏛️

NAAC A+ & Category-1 Autonomy

UGC mandates that only universities accredited with NAAC Grade 'A+' (CGPA 3.26+) or ranked in the Top-100 NIRF overall framework are entitled to offer online degrees without prior individual programme approvals.

⚖️

Permitted vs Restricted Fields

UGC permits Online degrees in MBA, BBA, MCA, BCA, B.Com, Data Science & MA. Engineering (B.Tech), Medicine, Nursing, Law (LL.B), and Pharmacy are strictly restricted in 100% online mode per council rules.

🛡️

UGC Fee Refund Mandate

UGC statutory notifications protect students with guaranteed fee refund rules (100% refund on pre-session cancellation) and mandate AI & Human Remote Proctored online semester examinations.
